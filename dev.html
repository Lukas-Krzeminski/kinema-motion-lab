Drop-sync verification

Runs the real build loop against the real audio engine and measures how long the bar takes to reach 100%. Every run must land on the same audio position — that is what guarantees the reveal hits the beat.

Idle.

Tuning DROP_TIME_SEC: play your track, note the timestamp of the beat you want the bar to finish on, and set AUDIO.DROP_TIME_SEC in src/config.js. The build screen also has a live sync scope — press the backtick key while it runs.
